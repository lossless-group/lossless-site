# A New Icon, a Keeping Up Page Worth Scanning, and Four Retired Collections

## Why Care?

Three things you'll notice on [lossless.group](https://lossless.group). The browser tab shows our redesigned record icon, and the header trademark is rebuilt around it. The [Keeping Up](https://lossless.group/keeping-up) page, where we track what changed in the tools we use, went from a wall of text to something you can scan: a logo for each tool, a one-line lede, real tag chips, and the date the release actually happened.

The third is mostly invisible, which is the point. For two years the site published our internal engineering notes (prompts for AI code assistants, issue write-ups, reminders, blueprints) as public collections. Those notes now live in the `context-v/` folder of the repo each one is about, where the agents working in that repo can find them. Four collections came off the site, and every one of their old URLs redirects somewhere sensible instead of 404ing.

## What's New?

- **The v3 icon.** A full favicon set (SVG, ICO, Apple touch icon, 192 and 512 PNGs, web manifest) and a header trademark rebuilt with the new mark. The old files are still there; nothing was overwritten.
- **Keeping Up.** Each post shows the tool's favicon, its lede and its tags as the site's standard tag chips, dated by `release_date`. Still a list, newest first, because recency is the point of the page. 105 posts got ledes and 58 got favicons to make this work.
- **Changelog dropdown.** One big button to the [Lossless Changelog](https://changelog.lossless.group/), wearing that site's favicon; every other option is a small text link.
- **Vibe Code with Us.** The [page](https://lossless.group/vibe-with/us) and the homepage section now show the context-v flow diagram from the [Context-V Corpus](https://lossless-group.github.io/context-v-corpus/) splash, which just gained a handoffs step: the note one session leaves so the next can pick the work back up.
- **Footer.** Vibe Coding Resources now points to [context-v.dev](https://context-v.dev), the Context-V Corpus and Specifications. Reminders and Prompts are gone with their collections.
- **Explorations open.** Exploration detail pages had always returned 404 because the collection had no route mapping. They resolve now.

## Retiring Four Collections Without Breaking Links

| Collection | Where its documents went | Old URLs now go to |
|---|---|---|
| blueprints | this repo's `context-v/blueprints` (12 of 13; one superseded copy dropped) | `/vibe-with/us` |
| reminders | `context-v/reminders` across the tree | `/vibe-with/us` |
| issue-resolution | `context-v/issues`, mostly the site and tidyverse | `/lost-in-public` |
| prompts | `context-v/prompts`; 50 of 93 came to this repo | `/vibe-with/us` |

The hard part was finding every URL. Removing a collection removes its pages, but the site's `[...path]` catch-all had also been serving the same documents at bare paths like `/some-prompt-slug`, and those never showed up in the collection's own route list. So we diffed the live sitemap against a fresh build plus the redirect map, and anything live today but missing from the build got a redirect. That found 75 bare-path URLs the first pass had missed. All told, 349 redirects went into `src/utils/routing/redirects.mjs`.

The site's own `context-v/` took the largest share of the moved documents, roughly a hundred, each given OKF frontmatter (`type`, `hex_code`, `site_uuid`) and an index line on arrival.

## Under the Hood

- **The diagram.** `ContextVFlow.astro` renders a snapshot of the corpus splash's Archify diagram, with its links pointed at the splash and its own dark-palette tokens. The edge labels ("1 → many", "next session") were black on dark at first; they now have their own styles.
- **pnpm 12.** The build failed with `ERR_PNPM_VERIFY_DEPS_BEFORE_RUN` because pnpm 12 no longer reads settings from `package.json`. The `pnpm` field moved to `pnpm-workspace.yaml`.
- **Header dropdown.** `JumboDropdown.astro` learned a `primary` item (the solid button) and a `compact` mode (title-only links). The trigger stays a button.
- **Tag chips on Keeping Up** render outside each post's link, because `TagChip`'s click handler calls `preventDefault` and would otherwise swallow the click.

## What's Next

- The `site_uuid` copies in `src/generated-content` and the Context-V Corpus still carry pre-move paths until their next regeneration.
- The empty `mdx-pages` collection still warns on every build; it's a one-line removal.
