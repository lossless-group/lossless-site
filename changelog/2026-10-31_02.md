## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit gives every doc a small baseline of frontmatter
facts: a title, its dates, its authors, and two IDs, a `site_uuid` and a
six-character `hex_code`. The IDs are what citations, roll-ups, and the corpus
use to follow a doc through moves and renames.

The five docs written in October already carried the whole template. The four
written earlier (the rebuild ledger and handoff, and two explorations) had
everything except the IDs.

## What's New?

- Four docs gain a `site_uuid` and a `hex_code`:
  - `decisions/2026-08-03_Rebuild-Keep-Drop-Ledger.md`
  - `explorations/Abandoned-Intentions-Tube-Visual.md`
  - `explorations/Rethink-on-Client-Focused-Landing-Pages.md`
  - `handoffs/2026-08-04_Rebuild-Kickoff-Handoff.md`
- Nothing else needed normalizing: statuses and tags were already Title-Case,
  and authors, tools, and tags were already lists.

## Under the Hood

Each ID was minted by command (`uuidgen`, `/dev/urandom`) and checked against
the whole Lossless tree for duplicates before it was pasted in by hand. No
date, version, status, or body line changed. The four docs keep their
`semantic_version:` key as written; it's a permanent alias of
`at_semantic_version`.
