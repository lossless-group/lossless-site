## Why Care?

Long articles were hard to scan. Body text was 14.4px with tight 1.3 line
spacing, so headings towered over it, and on pages that use `#` for sections the
section headings shrank to body size. Citations printed six-character hex codes
like `[fh0on7]`, and on pages where a citation had lost its source, the raw
`[^fh0on7]` text showed mid-sentence.

## What's New?

- **A relative type scale for every article.** Body text is 18px (17px on
  phones) at 1.7 line spacing, and each heading level is 1.25× the one below
  (1.2× on phones). Page titles sit one step above section headings on every
  width. Follows astro-knots' `Relative-Type-Scale-for-LFM-Reading-Content`
  blueprint.
- **Citations are numbered on render.** Inline citations read `[1]`, `[2]`… in
  order of first appearance, and the Sources list uses the same numbers. The hex
  code stays in the markdown as the citation's identity, so reordering a page just
  renumbers.
- **Every source still shows.** Sources nothing cites are listed after the cited
  ones, and old `[2]: …` source lines that used to run together in one paragraph
  are now separate numbered entries.
- **Citations with no source go quiet.** They render as a faded, unlinked `[?]`
  instead of raw text. 111 content pages have these; the cleanup is tracked in
  cite-wide's `Footnote-References-Orphaned-From-Their-Definitions` issue.
- **Market pages have art and ledes.** All 44 Market Categories and five market
  maps gained Image-Gin banners and portraits, and every category card now opens
  with a one-line lede.

## Under the Hood

- `src/styles/reader.css` is copied from
  `astro-knots/packages/lfm-astro/components/reader.css`, with two marked
  additions for this site: a step for `h1` inside the body, and the title at r⁵.
  `OneArticleOnPage` wraps rendered markdown in `.reader`, gives the title
  `.reader-title`, and drops its old hand-typed rem sizes, which would have
  outranked the scale.
- `remark-footnote-numbers` follows lfm's `remark-lfm-citations` convention and
  runs after `remarkCitations` in all seven markdown pipelines. It keeps
  definitions where they are in the document and sorts each run of them by
  number.
- `src/generated-content` moves to `3a2b04c8` for the banners and ledes.
