## Why Care?

Marking a page `publish: false` didn't reliably take it off the site, because most routes never checked the flag. Fixing that, and reorganizing the venture firm directory, would have left 161 indexed URLs returning 404s. Those links now land on the right page or the nearest section.

## What's New?

- **`publish: false` works for every public collection.** Unpublished entries no longer get a page, a list entry, a tag count or a sitemap line. Client portals are exempt because they decide their own visibility.
- **111 moved pages redirect to their new address**, mostly venture firms now under `/toolkit/vertical/alternatives/`.
- **50 unpublished pages redirect to their nearest section**, so old links don't dead-end.

## Under the Hood

`publishedGlob` wraps Astro's glob loader, so the filter applies once at the collection level and a new route can't forget it. It filters entries as they're written as well as after loading, because two files can share a slug. An unpublished copy would otherwise overwrite its published twin and take it offline, as the Longevity market map showed.

The Vercel adapter writes redirect rules that match `/old` but not `/old/`, and every URL in our sitemap ends in a slash. `matchTrailingSlashOnRedirects` adjusts the rules after the adapter's build step so both forms match.

Wikilinks to an unpublished file resolve to `/404`, the same as a link to an unwritten note.

## Verification

We compared the 8,232 URLs in the live sitemap with a full build. Every one now has a page or a redirect, every redirect target exists, no rule catches a page that still exists, and there are no redirect chains. This was checked against the generated Vercel config, not on a live deployment.

## What's Next?

If an unpublished page is published again, remove its line from `src/utils/routing/redirects.mjs`.
